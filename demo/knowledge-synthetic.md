Resume review method

1. Read the top third first. If the target role is unclear from it within ten seconds, cap the score at 5.
2. Every bullet needs an action, a number and a result. Count the bullets that have all three. Under half caps the score at 6.
3. Duties are not achievements. Flag any bullet starting with "Responsible for".
4. Tools listed without evidence of use count for nothing.
5. Gaps over six months need one honest line. Hiding them is worse than the gap.
6. Two pages only with ten or more years of experience.
7. The most recent role should carry the most detail.
8. Fix order: target role, strongest proof, then wording.
